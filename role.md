## Job Profile Summary

### Primary Function

The **Innovation Developer** is responsible for assisting in the design, development, and implementation of innovative software solutions within the University's ICT framework. Reporting to the **Associate Director, Emerging Technology and Innovation**, this role supports the development of emerging technologies — such as AI, machine learning, cloud computing, and IoT solutions — to improve service delivery, operational efficiency, and system performance.

The Innovation Developer works collaboratively with the Emerging Technology and Innovation Team to prototype, test, and implement new software applications that align with the University's digital strategy. The role provides hands-on support in developing scalable solutions and ensures that all software is optimised to meet evolving business needs.

### Decision Making and Delegations of Authority

The Innovation Developer works under the guidance of the Associate Director, Emerging Technology and Innovation, exercising a degree of independence in determining the best approaches to software development and problem-solving. The role contributes to the design and implementation of software solutions and provides input on coding practices, testing, and optimising applications.

While major decisions regarding architecture and strategic alignment rest with the Senior Innovation Developer, this role involves substantial autonomy in development tasks and prototyping.

## Responsibilities

### 1. Software Development and Support

- Assist in the design and development of AI, machine learning, cloud-based platforms, and IoT applications to enhance the University's digital services and software infrastructure.
- Write clean, scalable, and efficient code to support ongoing projects.
- Implement best practices in software development, ensuring applications are secure, reliable, and optimised for performance.
- Contribute to specialised software development projects focused on enhancing digital services and innovation within the University's ICT portfolio.

### 2. Prototyping and Testing

- Support the Senior Innovation Developer in the development and testing of software prototypes to evaluate emerging technologies.
- Participate in trials of new software solutions, including AI-driven applications and cloud services, to assess their feasibility and scalability within the University's ICT environment.
- Help refine prototypes based on feedback and testing results.

### 3. Data Analysis and Integration

- Collaborate with the Emerging Technology and Innovation Team to integrate software solutions with existing data pipelines, analytics tools, and platforms.
- Utilise data-driven approaches to optimise application performance, ensuring that software solutions meet operational and strategic needs.

### 4. Technical Consultation and Collaboration

- Provide expert technical input during the development of software architecture and solution design, working closely with the Senior Innovation Developer.
- Liaise with internal ICT teams to ensure the smooth integration of new software solutions into existing infrastructure.
- Provide recommendations on coding standards, tools, and methods to improve software development processes.

### 5. Continuous Improvement and Development

- Provide subject matter expertise regarding the latest trends in AI, machine learning, IoT, and software development to support the development of cutting-edge solutions.
- Participate in continuous learning opportunities to expand knowledge of software development methodologies and emerging technologies.
- Provide advice and recommendations for improving existing applications and processes, ensuring alignment with University goals.

### 6. Ad-hoc Projects and Development Initiatives

- Perform work activities relevant to the role's key accountabilities as approved by the Manager and commensurate to the role's classification level.
- Assist with ad-hoc development projects as directed by the Senior Innovation Developer.
